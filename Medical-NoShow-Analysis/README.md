Medical Appointment No-Show Analysis

Author: Madeline Galbraith

Overview

This project analyzes a dataset containing more than 100,000 medical appointments in Brazil to identify factors associated with missed appointments. Using Python, exploratory data analysis, and statistical comparisons, the project examines patient behavior and identifies trends that may help healthcare organizations reduce appointment no-shows.

Business Problem

Missed medical appointments create scheduling inefficiencies, reduce provider productivity, and delay patient care. The goal of this project was to analyze historical appointment data and determine which factors were associated with patient attendance.

Research Questions

This analysis investigates the following questions:

Does the amount of time between scheduling and the appointment affect attendance?
Does receiving an SMS reminder appear to influence attendance?
Is patient age associated with appointment attendance?

Key Findings

The analysis identified several factors associated with appointment attendance:

Patients with longer wait times were more likely to miss their appointments.
Attendance rates differed between patients who received SMS reminders and those who did not.
Patients who attended appointments were slightly older, on average, than patients who missed them.

Because this was an observational dataset, the analysis identifies associations rather than cause-and-effect relationships.

Business Recommendation

Healthcare organizations may benefit from reducing appointment wait times and monitoring reminder strategies to improve attendance rates. These findings can help support scheduling decisions and future predictive analytics initiatives focused on reducing patient no-shows.
